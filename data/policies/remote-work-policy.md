title: Remote Work Policy
source: sharepoint://contoso/policies/remote-work.docx
allowed_groups: all-employees
---
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Remote work outside your home country requires HR approval and is limited to 20 working days per year.
A one-time home office allowance of 400 USD is available for a desk, chair or monitor.
All remote work must use a company-managed device connected through the GlobalConnect VPN.
