Employees must book business travel through the Contoso travel portal at least 14 days in advance.
The hotel limit is 180 USD per night in standard cities and 260 USD per night in high-cost cities such as London, New York and Tokyo.
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
Meal expenses are reimbursed up to 75 USD per day with itemised receipts.
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of returning from a trip.
