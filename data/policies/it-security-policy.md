Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are reset through the self-service portal at aka.ms/sspr.
Multi-factor authentication with Microsoft Authenticator is mandatory for all accounts.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 1 hour.
Only software from the Company Portal may be installed on company devices.
The GlobalConnect 6 VPN client replaces the old VPN client on 1 December.
