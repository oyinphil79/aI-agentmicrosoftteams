Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year plus public holidays.
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.
Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for all parents after 6 months of service.
Sick leave of more than 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
